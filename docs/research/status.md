# Evaluation status

Simjecture provides an autonomous research loop and a recorded evidence system.
Its documented studies demonstrate specific workflows and numerical outcomes;
they do not establish unrestricted scientific reliability. Simjecture was first
built to attack simulation-gated plasma conjectures. The
claim ledger, sandbox, commissioning protocol, and capability framework are
now domain-neutral enough to extend to other fields that have a sharp
question, a commissioned instrument, and a cheap verification path. Plasma
remains the origin domain.

## Demonstrated

- Natural-language hypothesis input and autonomous agent-authored computation.
- A current minimal-mode [Kepler counterexample-and-repair study](../demos/kepler-energy.md)
  with accepted reviews, preserved arrays and independent numerical reproduction.
- Domain-neutral claims, prospective evidence contracts, and guarded closure.
- Sandboxed Python plus separately installed CPU and CUDA capabilities.
- Instrument commissioning across representation, physics, boundaries,
  diagnostics, and numerical regime.
- Recorded program, input, command, artifact and claim provenance, with model
  usage reporting and explicit incomplete-usage flags.
- Durable long-running execution, cancellation, replay, and recovery.
- A planted matched-moment kinetic-stability counterexample verified by analytic,
  independent PIC, and WarpX paths.
- Real magnetic-mirror, nonlinear-Landau, Gray–Scott, and two-dimensional
  collisionless GEM autonomous campaigns, including a preserved 12-run CUDA
  [GEM demonstration](../demos/collisionless-gem.md).

The [fresh FLASH island-coalescence audit](../demos/island-coalescence.md)
also preserves an unresolved outcome and a post-run correction: a half-cell error
in the agent's diagnostic caused false eligibility rejections and was missed in
methods review. Recorded decisions are inspectable judgments, not automatic
scientific correctness.

The [two-solver FLASH-to-WarpX study](../demos/flash-warpx-patch.md) also completed
real state transfer and numerical controls while leaving its claim unresolved.
The [demo refresh audit](demo-audit-20261008.md) records the observed agent errors,
harness fixes and remaining limitations separately.

## Not demonstrated

- Reliable execution of arbitrary acceptable natural-language hypotheses.
- Empirical truth beyond the installed simulation model family.
- Consistently correct autonomous statistical interpretation.
- A model advantage over equal-budget conventional scientific strategies.

## Next

The current target remains methods and infrastructure. The same claim
ledger, sandbox, commissioning protocol, and capability framework are ready
to extend to other simulation-gated fields. The next scientific step is to
use that tooling to hunt independently confirmed new results — a compact
law, a matched counterexample, or a scoped impossibility — in the origin
domain and beyond.
