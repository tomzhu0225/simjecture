# Autonomous collisionless GEM run 0004

Run 0004 is the strongest completed end-to-end plasma stress test in the 0.1
research preview. It was a guided autonomous campaign: the operator supplied a
validated, non-evidentiary CUDA/openPMD starting instrument, and the agent chose
and froze the held-out experiment and analysis protocol.

## Root hypothesis

In a fixed two-dimensional, non-relativistic, fully kinetic, collisionless GEM
Harris-sheet regime at reduced mass ratio 25, the seed-ensemble mean late-window
normalized flux-slope rate at $T_i/T_e=1$ is at least 25 percent larger than at
$T_i/T_e=20$, and the contrast persists under a particle-count control.

## Autonomous execution

- 75 model iterations over approximately 177 minutes;
- a commissioned CUDA WarpX simulator and CPU ensemble analyzer;
- 12 fresh scientific CUDA simulations;
- three paired held-out seeds, two temperature ratios, and two particle counts;
- 256 by 128 cells, four fully kinetic populations, 4,623 explicit
  Yee/Esirkepov steps, and 26 openPMD field states per run;
- all simulator summaries passed their 26 declared five-aspect checks;
- maximum absolute combined energy drift below $4.85\times10^{-4}$.

The discovery seed from the prior campaign was excluded.

## Recorded result

At 16 particles per cell per population, the endpoint means were approximately
0.07541 and 0.07450, giving $R_{16}=1.0122$. At 8 particles per cell they were
approximately 0.05339 and 0.04917, giving $R_8=1.0858$.

The frozen point-estimate rule marked the operational child claim falsified
because all groups were valid and $R_{16}<1.25$. The paired three-seed bootstrap
interval for $R_{16}$ was $[0.823,1.437]$, however, and therefore still included
the proposed population threshold. The lower-particle control was highly
seed-sensitive.

## Audit interpretation

The campaign ran nominally and produced a legitimate finite-sample protocol
outcome. Its terminal prose strengthened that outcome into a population-level
falsification and described the particle-count control too confidently. Those
are plausible scientific-judgment errors rather than missing computation or
fabricated evidence.

The ledger preserved the operational child's contracted falsification while the
immutable root remained open. The defensible external interpretation is:

> The observed sample means do not show a 25 percent enhancement. The specified
> point-estimate protocol falsified its operational child, while the population
> root remains unresolved with three seeds.
