# Video treatment: Can an AI researcher explain a Z-pinch energy puzzle?

Suggested duration: 12–16 minutes. This is an evidence-led outline, not a finished
script or a claim of discovery. The campaign was still running at the retrospective
cutoff; record a final-status card before publishing.

## 1. The question, 0:00–1:30

Show an original diagram of an imploding plasma annulus and axial current. Explain
the apparent puzzle: integrated radiation can exceed the shell's peak inward
kinetic energy. These are different quantities. Electrical work and initially
stored magnetic energy must be included before invoking missing physics.

State the real ambition: identify and test a mechanism, not merely produce a ratio
above one. Do not suggest that our simplified aluminium setup reproduces a specific
experimental shot or tungsten spectrum.

## 2. Building an experiment the agent can run, 1:30–3:30

Show the actual RZ geometry, the two annuli, current waveform, and material model.
Explain why RZ is useful and what it excludes. Show the commissioning table,
including the existing 3D runs and their limitations. Separate “program finished”
from “scientific result qualified.”

## 3. The first apparent success, 3:30–5:30

Plot measured radiation and peak kinetic energy using saved artifacts. Annotate
the integration interval. The first campaign's 20 ns and 40 ns values illustrate
why time-window choice matters; label them unaccepted model results.

Explain why this does not yet isolate trailing-plasma current redistribution.
Include the no-trailing control rather than hide the inconvenient result.

## 4. Human review changes the next experiment, 5:30–8:00

Display the dated operator steering and the four-case comparison figure. Explain
the two interventions: move trailing material; hold the current after 6 ns.
Highlight the small response to the late-drive change and the modest response to
geometry. Holding current is not power-off.

Keep the correction in the story: we initially placed too much emphasis on a hard
spectral cutoff and narrow time window. The user's challenge corrected that.
Human involvement is part of the method, not something edited out to make the
system look fully autonomous.

## 5. Where the explanation breaks, 8:00–10:30

Show a control-volume diagram and the internal versus near-boundary residual.
Explain the stored-radiation double count and the current mismatch. Distinguish
the bookkeeping mistakes that were corrected from the closure problem that remains.

Show the 3D failure as a missing sandbox input path, not a FLASH physics failure.
Contrast the successful earlier commissioning environment with the failed recorded
submission. Avoid implying that no 3D solver was available.

## 6. The cost of activity without enough evidence, 10:30–12:30

Show recorded token totals, with cache and incomplete-accounting caveats, and the
1 h 53 min reconnect-wait counter. User-reported RMB 100 spent is not an audited
per-stage invoice; the second RMB 100 is a top-up, not known additional spending.

Show the sanitized 402 Insufficient Balance message. The system continued trying
because it treated the condition as a disconnect. Explain what the observer should
have seen in the GUI. Do not equate reviewer attempts with completed reviews.

## 7. What we know, and the next defensible experiment, 12:30–15:00

End with three explicit statements:

- We obtained reproducible RZ broadband excess in this model family.
- We have not established a validated experimental explanation or a qualified 3D mechanism.
- The next step is verified drive and energy accounting, then a discriminating
  mechanism test and qualified 3D comparison—not another blind long run.

Show the repair priorities and a frozen evidence bundle. Present this as an honest
research attempt that produced both useful comparisons and costly process failures.

## Production checklist

- Regenerate plots from frozen numeric artifacts; retain units and observation windows.
- Label reconstructed diagrams, scratch pilots, accepted evidence, and exploratory results.
- Redact keys, passwords, host addresses, request IDs, and private filesystem details.
- Use original diagrams or licensed paper figures; verify experimental quotations against sources.
- Preserve steering timestamps and disclose human intervention.
- Update final campaign status and costs before recording the closing narration.
