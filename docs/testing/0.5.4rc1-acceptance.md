# 0.5.4rc1 scope, checks and limits

This preview adds execution strategy control to minimal research. The worker
chooses its scientific approach; an independent director can stop named
experiments and request a recorded replacement plan or reasoned challenge.
Methods approval and scientific claim acceptance remain separate reviews.

## Included behavior

- A default-on director for new minimal studies, with visible GUI launch settings,
  model/effort route, decisions, stop confirmations and worker responses. CLI users
  can select `--director` or `--no-director`. Existing studies retain their saved policy.
- Strategy checks during long experiment waits and sustained planning, bounded
  director calls, named cancellation and available partial-artifact retention.
  A later continue review cannot waive the latest unacknowledged replan. A failed
  stop control remains visible without losing the valid directive or preventing
  other requested stops. Recovered reviews clear stale review errors.
- Optional bounded JSON/FLASH live timing through local and SSH execution,
  receipt-backed advisory progress, measured cost and conditional runtime estimates.
  Older workers remain usable without monitoring; advertised support is required
  when a new experiment requests it.
- Reserved-slot MPI launch helpers, read-only compiler alternatives and dependency
  source manifests for OverlayFS hosts with unstable inode numbers. Explicit trusted
  `process-cooperative` execution is selectable in CLI/TUI/browser machine settings.
- Frozen exploratory analysis, clearer method experiment-reference validation,
  separate descriptive limitations and native reviewer read-only tool events.
- GUI discovery of externally launched studies without a server restart.

## Verification

Release verification is recorded here before publication. The four new recovery
regressions and the existing director suite pass: **18 tests**. The release also
retains the earlier [execution qualification](zpinch-repair-qualification-20261003.md)
and [director/adaptive commissioning report](research-director-adaptive-20261003.md),
which distinguish launcher readiness from scientific accuracy.

The ongoing 12-hour Al campaign has completed unperturbed and coherently perturbed
48×48×24 Cartesian FLASH trajectories through 40 ns, each in approximately
51 minutes. The director stopped a larger, poorly specified stochastic follow-up
and the worker implemented a replacement. These are operational observations from
an unfinished campaign, not a benchmark of scientific completion or model cost.
The candidate's additional recovery changes are verified separately; this running
campaign continues on its original private deployment.

## Limits

- The campaign has no accepted methods or reconnection attribution result at the
  recorded checkpoint. Spatial resolution, temporal accuracy, energy accounting
  and kinetic-reference uncertainty still require qualification. Small changes in
  radiation output between perturbations do not measure direct reconnection energy.
- The broad adaptive temporal screen failed despite completing all commissioned
  trajectories. Its failed results remain preserved. FLASH guidance prefers
  qualified adaptive stepping and affordable complete exploration, not arbitrarily
  large forced timesteps.
- Director calls can time out and add substantial context/tool overhead. Native
  usage counters distinguish worker/director roles and cache usage, but do not
  supply provider invoices or complete per-request counts. This preview does not
  claim reduced spending or guarantee useful scientific judgment.
- Linear completion forecasts can be misleading during startup ramps or changing
  plasma conditions. They are mutable operational telemetry, not evidence.
- Cooperative process and PRoot execution are not OS security boundaries. Native
  agent host tools remain available. Source manifests verify declared bytes; they
  do not qualify the solver's numerical method.
- No new model leaderboard edition, full WarpX patch investigation or experimental
  reproduction is claimed. Structured and frontier supervision retain their
  existing behavior.
