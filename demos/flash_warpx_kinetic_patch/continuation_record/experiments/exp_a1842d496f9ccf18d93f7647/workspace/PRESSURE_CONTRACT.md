## Operational qualification amendment v2

This amendment responds to methods review before any decisive evidence. It does not alter the source, transferred physics, measurement region, observable, full window or 0.20 claim bound.

Initialization requires density bin RMS <=3%, scalar-pressure bin RMS and mean errors <=3%, each against the exact fractional cell/bin overlap reference. Independently form the measured mass-weighted species center-of-mass velocity and compare it with the overlap-weighted transferred center-of-mass flow; vector RMS error <=0.03 v0. Species drifts and current RMS are reported separately. No source physics is adjusted to satisfy these checks.

Secondary 8×8 bins require count>=128, effective count>=256 and finite positive pressure at every sample used for shear reconstruction. Ineligible secondary bins make that shear check inconclusive. Reconstruct the 4×4 tensor from its four sub-bins using their pressure, density and mean velocities; relative tensor RMS reconstruction discrepancy <=1e-10 is an algebraic diagnostic check. Measured between-sub-bin velocity covariance must contribute <=0.02 of the primary pressure normalization. This bounds measured broadening between sub-bins only; within-sub-bin shear remains unresolved. The grid comparison and the explicit finite-bin convention retain that limitation.

Transfer relaxation is accepted only if both species' mean scalar pressure and density differ by <=5% between sampled [0.2,0.3) and [0.3,0.4) intervals, the area-mean A differs by <=0.03 between the two halves [0.3,0.55) and [0.55,0.8], and interior electric/thermal energy <=0.01. These operational plateau/energy tests distinguish a large persistent initialization disturbance from the conditioned measurement; they do not prove equilibrium. Failure means the fixed window is inconclusive. Elapsed initialization time alone cannot pass the gate.

Kinetic-scale accuracy requires a complete same-physics spatial comparison through 0.8, with absolute mean A changes <=0.02 for each species, scalar pressure differences <=5%, density differences <=3%, and no accounted-energy residual >0.005. A complete timestep-halving comparison must give mean A changes <=0.01 and pressure differences <=3%. Particle-count/seed changes enter the uncertainty envelope and must remain <=0.03 in A. Debye-underresolved grids can qualify this finite pressure observable only through these measured accuracy and energy controls; they are never called generally Debye-resolved. Missing controls leave the result inconclusive. Spatial comparison is resolution bounded, not an asymptotic proof.

Energy totals must use the same step/time for field and particle diagnostics, cumulative absorbed energy and flux history. Evaluate the last jointly available complete sample, not unmatched final arrays. Relative accounted residual must be <=0.005; independently integrate every other boundary-flux sample over identical endpoints and require normalized quadrature change <=0.001. Native particle energy and in-situ live energy at that step must agree within 0.002 of initial total. Nearest-cell spatial flux quadrature remains an approximation; compare its budget under grid/timestep controls and retain the residual. These checks bound gross numerical source/loss behavior for the finite observable, not exact physical conservation.
