Energy accounting includes actual absorbed-particle energy and sampled nearest-cell boundary Poynting flux; the estimate and its residual must be reported and checked under controls. No closed budget is claimed when its quadrature is inadequate.

Uncertainty/decision: retain the complete control envelope. Per-species upper uncertainty is a sampling allowance of at least 0.05 (two-sigma conservative scale from at least 512 effective particles per bin, with measured fourth-moment/seed checks), plus absolute changes in the window mean A from the spatial, timestep, particle-count, seed, patch and bin/cadence controls, plus a relativistic-convention bound sqrt(3) times the maximum measured energy-weighted gamma-1 trace correction. Controls are conditional numerical error checks, not a rigorous distribution-free confidence theorem. Support requires the upper endpoint <=0.20 for both species and all qualification gates. Falsification requires a lower endpoint >0.20 for at least one species with every gate qualified. Otherwise inconclusive. Any numerical, boundary or relaxation failure invalidates a scientific counterexample. No threshold, region or shorter window will be fitted to decisive output.
