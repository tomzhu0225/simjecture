For the planar Kepler problem with GM=1 and semimajor axis a=1, a fixed-step kick-drift-kick leapfrog integration whose maximum sampled relative energy error stays below 0.001 also keeps its maximum position error below 0.01a over twenty orbital periods. Test eccentricities e in {0, 0.3, 0.6} and {64, 128, 256, 512} equal timesteps per orbital period, starting at periapsis. Position error is measured against the exact Kepler orbit at the same physical time.
