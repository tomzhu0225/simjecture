◐ Interactive

              
Explore with your agent

              
Chat, run code and simulations, look at plots and decide the next step together. You stay in the loop.

              
Best for getting started, debugging, and working out what to ask.

              

                
                
              

            

            

              
◎ Autonomous study

              
Let a study run on its own

              
Give it a claim and a time budget. It runs experiments and looks for counterexamples; an independent review checks the evidence before anything counts.

              
Best for “is this true?” questions you can state precisely.

              

                
              

              
Start in Interactive; when your claim is clear, switch to Autonomous study inside the conversation.
