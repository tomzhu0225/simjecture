# Bilingual documentary storyboard

Twelve matching narrative beats for both languages. Each beat has two layout phases, aligned captions and an in-scene dissolve. Exact audio durations and chapter starts are generated from the stock-voice recordings, not estimated reading speed.

## 01-motivation — From fluent answers to usable evidence / 从回答问题，到交付可用的证据

Introduce the practical gap between fluent explanation and reproducible evidence. Show the scope and 42/252/2 count cards.

---

## 02-tasks — Two tasks, two deadlines / 两项任务，两种时间预算

Show CSV and HDF5 task cards, original deadlines and a clearly labelled cylindrical grid schematic.

---

## 03-protocol — Measure the whole coding agent / 测量完整的编码代理配置

Distinguish scheduled, availability-only, and graded attempts. Explain immutable inputs, independent rerun and three numerical holdouts.

---

## 04-short — Correct numbers did not guarantee completion / 数值正确，仍然可能没有完成任务

Contrast aggregate numerical and full-delivery passes, then show selected configurations with paired pass-count bars.

---

## 05-short-results — Read completion rate alongside speed / 完成率和速度要放在一起看

Show three configurations with their successful median times and denominators; qualify five-trial uncertainty.

---

## 06-rz — A longer diagnostic task changed the picture / 更长的诊断任务，呈现出不同结果

Show 34/40 overall and 19/19 Codex outcomes, then a top-ten finish-time subset. Keep n=1 and task/budget confounding visible.

---

## 07-adapters — A result belongs to a model–agent configuration / 结果属于模型和代理的完整配置

Contrast numeric checks with delivery for MiMo/Grok; explain whole-agent configuration and tool-history differences.

---

## 08-bars — Two rankings, with failure kept visible / 时间榜和成本榜，都保留失败状态

Show independently sorted time and cost bars. Explain red unfinished status, visible recorded spending, partial receipts and denominators; omit no-inference configurations from public rankings.

---

## 09-pareto — Time and money: the upper left is better / 时间与费用：左上角更好

Show all eligible RZ cost/time points, reversed time axis and logarithmic money axis. Highlight observed frontier points with independent white rings; colours identify models and dashed connections only join same-model/same-agent efforts.

---

## 10-costs — A public price needs trustworthy usage counters / 公开单价，还需要可信的用量记录

Explain complete usage, estimates and lower bounds, standard cache-adjusted tariffs, and cumulative receipt pitfalls.

---

## 11-verifier — The benchmark itself needed scientific controls / 基准测试本身也需要独立对照

Show the 0.1/0.2 reference defects and 0.3 repair, independent controls and separation of qualification records.

---

## 12-outlook — A reproducible starting point for scientific AI / 为科学 AI 留下可复现的起点

Separate tested diagnostics from future solver/hypothesis evaluations. End with source branch, reproducibility, community contributions and synthetic-voice disclosure.
